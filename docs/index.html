01 Who we are

Speed Dock is an independent Android productivity application developed and published by CAPSD. This page explains, in plain language, how the app handles information.

It applies to the Speed Dock app downloaded from Google Play and any updates to it. Effective date: 30 September 2026.

Privacy questions? Write to us any time at catamilselvan2001@gmail.com — we answer every message.

02 The short version

Speed Dock is privacy focused by design. The app works entirely offline for its core features, requires no account, no login and no personal details, and the developer does not collect, receive, store, sell or share any personal data from users.

We have no backend service, no user database and no analytics of our own. There is literally nothing about you on our side — so there is nothing for us to leak, sell or hand over.

Where the app does reach the internet (for example to download an on‑device language model, or to display an ad supplied by a third party), those requests are made by well‑known Google services under their own privacy policies — never to a CAPSD server, because we do not operate one.

03 What CAPSD doesn't collect

The following is a complete list of what we, the developer, do not collect:

Name or username Email address Phone number Contacts Location / GPS Photos or media Call or SMS logs Device advertising ID IP address Usage or analytics data Crash reports Financial or payment data

We also do not build profiles, do not run behavioural advertising of our own, and do not rent, sell, trade or otherwise transfer your information to anyone — because we never receive it in the first place.

Scope note: the list above covers data collected by CAPSD. Data processed by the third‑party services listed in section 06 — such as advertising identifiers used to serve ads — is handled by those providers directly, under their own privacy policies, and never reaches us.

04 What stays on your device

Speed Dock's features read or write information so that it can work for you. That data is processed locally and is never transmitted to us:

  • Your content — notes, text snippets, favourites, theme and layout preferences.
  • Productivity state — timers, stopwatch, pomodoro and alarm sessions.
  • Screen text you choose to capture — OCR and translation run on‑device; text you scan is not sent anywhere by us.
  • System information — battery, RAM, CPU, storage and network stats shown in the app's dashboards are read locally for display only.
  • Accessibility & notification features — used strictly to deliver the on‑screen dock, gestures and the controls you enable. This data is not uploaded.
No account or special access required. Speed Dock has no sign‑in, username, password, 2‑step verification, QR code, membership or biometric gate — every user can access every feature immediately after installing the app.
If you stop using a feature, the related local data simply sits on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes it entirely.

05 Permissions we use

Each Android permission the app requests has a direct, user‑initiated purpose. None is used to profile you.

Accessibility serviceDraws the speed dock overlay, performs your configured gestures and shortcuts. Content is handled in memory on your device and is never recorded or uploaded.
Notification accessOptional. Only used to show and manage notifications inside the dock's notification panel. You can revoke it at any time in Android settings.
CameraOptional. Used only when you start OCR, translation or colour analysis. The preview is processed on your device and no image is stored or transmitted by us.
Display over other appsLets the floating dock and overlays appear above other apps, as intended.
Post notificationsShows the app's own timer, pomodoro and reminder notifications. No data leaves the device.
Network state / InternetUsed for the features described in section 06 — never to report on you.
Vibrate, alarms, Do Not DisturbPurely functional: alerts, timer tones and focus modes you explicitly turn on.

07 Advertising

Speed Dock may show ads to keep the app free. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which uses cookies-like identifiers to deliver and measure advertising in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

  • The developer does not receive, collect or store your advertising identifier.
  • The developer does not sell, share or act as a data broker for ad data.
  • You can opt out of personalised ads through Google's Ads Settings and the Google Play services controls on your device.

08 Purchases & refunds

Premium unlocks and passes are bought through Google Play. Payment details (card, UPI, wallet) are entered and processed by Google — never by CAPSD. We only receive confirmation that a purchase succeeded, together with your order reference, in order to unlock the feature you bought.

Refunds are handled inside the Google Play refund window. Full details, including how to request a refund, are on our Refund Policy page.

09 Children's privacy

Speed Dock is a general‑audience tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). Because we collect no personal information at all, we do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through any channel, contact us and we will remove it promptly.

10 Retention & deletion

  • We retain nothing — there is no CAPSD database containing user records.
  • App data lives in your phone's private storage until you clear it or uninstall the app.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its local data.
  • Purchase records are retained by Google Play as required for receipts and refunds.
Want everything gone? Uninstall the app — that is the complete deletion process.

11 Your rights

Under regulations such as the GDPR and CCPA you have the right to access, correct, export or erase personal data held about you, and to object to its processing.

Since CAPSD holds no personal data, there is nothing to access, correct or erase on our side. If you would still like confirmation or help exercising a right, simply email us and we will respond within 30 days.

You can additionally control permissions at any time from Settings → Apps → Speed Dock → Permissions, and revoke the accessibility or notification access from Android's special‑access settings.

12 Policy changes

If the app's functionality ever changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and revise the effective date above. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy; if you do not agree, please stop using the app and uninstall it.
