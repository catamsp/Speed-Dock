01 Refunds

Speed Dock is distributed through the Google Play Store, and all transactions are processed by Google. Purchases and refunds are handled by Google per its standard terms and the Google Play refund window (typically within 48 hours of purchase).

To request a refund, open the Google Play Store, go to Account → Purchase history, select the transaction and choose Report a problem — or visit pay.google.com, open Activity and report the order. Google will review the request under its refund policy and your local consumer law.

This policy does not affect any rights you have under the consumer protection laws of your country.

02 Subscriptions & cancellation

You may cancel any subscription at any time through the Google Play Store (Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions). Your access will remain active until the end of the current billing period, and the subscription will not renew afterwards.

Refund requests for the current billing period are handled by Google within the Play refund window, as described above.

03 Purchase issues

If you have any issues with a purchase or refund, please contact us at the email below and we will do our best to assist you. Including your Google Play order reference helps us respond faster.

Refunds are issued by Google Play to the original payment method; timing then depends on your bank, card issuer or wallet provider. Once a refund is completed, any premium unlock tied to that purchase is deactivated in the app.
